# BLACK WEALTH EXCHANGE

## 12-MONTH REVENUE-FIRST GOVERNING DIRECTIVE

### Effective June 28, 2026 through June 30, 2027

Owner directive issued by Thomas Hooker, Founder and Chief Executive Officer, Black Wealth Exchange.

This directive supersedes all previous general expansion priorities, feature-roadmap assumptions, multi-brand plans, and instructions that conflict with it.

## 1. OWNER DECISION

For the next 12 months, Black Wealth Exchange is the only active business-building priority outside regular employment.

Books, entertainment properties, Millianious, Pamfa, The Last Nephilim, Tiana Song Sprouts, Guardians of the Forgotten Realm, music concepts, additional agent systems, and other business ideas remain protected assets, but they are not active development priorities during this period.

Do not initiate, recommend, schedule, or build work for those projects unless a new explicit owner instruction is given or the work directly produces measurable BWE revenue.

BlackForge’s active responsibility is Black Wealth Exchange.

## 2. THE NEW BWE MISSION

Black Wealth Exchange will become the trusted customer-acquisition and business-growth platform for Black-owned businesses.

BWE will begin by helping businesses:

- claim and verify their business identity;
- improve their public business profile;
- become easier for customers to discover;
- purchase measurable promotional visibility;
- receive customer leads and actions;
- understand campaign and profile performance;
- improve their business-growth readiness;
- receive paid growth and capital-readiness services;
- document measurable outcomes.

Discovery is the entry point.

Measurable business growth is the product.

Paying customers, retained customers, and documented outcomes are the proof.

## 3. THE PRIMARY 12-MONTH GOAL

By June 30, 2027, BWE must become a real, repeatable, revenue-producing company, not simply a large functioning platform.

Primary operating targets:

- at least $300,000 in collected BWE revenue during the 12-month period;
- stretch target of $500,000 in collected BWE revenue;
- at least $25,000 in recurring or contractually committed monthly revenue by the end of the period;
- at least 250 active paying business customers;
- at least 12 paid sponsor or institutional relationships;
- at least 36 completed paid business-growth or capital-readiness engagements;
- at least 70% 90-day retention among recurring paying business customers;
- at least 100 documented business-customer outcomes;
- one documented sales, onboarding, fulfillment, reporting, support, and renewal system that another qualified person can operate.

Collected BWE revenue means money actually paid to and retained by BWE. It does not mean marketplace GMV, unpaid Stripe Checkout Sessions, pending orders, projected seller payouts, directory inventory, database records, estimated sponsorship value, unpaid invoices, verbal commitments, or platform traffic without a financial conversion.

## 4. THE THREE AUTHORIZED PRIMARY OFFERS

### Offer One: BWE Verified Business Growth Membership

Initial working offer structure:

- Founding Verified Growth Membership: $49 per month or $499 per year;
- Growth Pro Membership: $99 per month or $999 per year.

Benefits must be clear, deliverable, measurable, and connected to real platform capability. Do not create benefits BWE cannot consistently fulfill.

### Offer Two: BWE Sponsored Visibility Campaigns

Initial working campaign levels:

- Local or Category Campaign: starting at $1,500;
- Growth Campaign: starting at $5,000;
- Institutional or Custom Campaign: $10,000 or more.

Every sponsor campaign must define campaign purpose, intended audience, included placements, supported businesses or categories, campaign dates, impressions or visibility evidence, clicks, leads or actions, fulfillment status, exceptions or delivery problems, final performance report, and renewal opportunity.

BWE will not sell vague exposure without proof of fulfillment.

### Offer Three: BWE Business Growth and Capital-Readiness Services

Initial working services:

- Premium Readiness Review: $499;
- Growth or Capital-Readiness Package: starting at $2,500;
- Institutional or Sponsored Business Cohort: custom pricing.

BWE must not promise funding, investment, loans, grants, contracts, or investor introductions. Any future regulated capital activity remains outside this 12-month build unless separately approved after legal review.

## 5. WHAT BWE IS NOT DOING DURING THIS PERIOD

Frozen from new expansion unless directly necessary to sell, deliver, retain, support, or measure one of the three approved offers:

- Travel Map expansion;
- Wealth Builder expansion;
- Black Card expansion;
- creator or music platform development;
- new social-network functionality;
- new entertainment lanes;
- new publishing integrations;
- new investment or private-equity functionality;
- new lending or securities functionality;
- broad marketplace feature expansion;
- broad jobs-platform expansion;
- advanced mapping or geolocation expansion;
- new cities selected without actual inventory and demand evidence;
- new dashboards that do not change a revenue decision;
- decorative admin tooling;
- speculative artificial-intelligence features;
- unrelated architecture modernization;
- large-scale visual redesigns;
- new brands or product families;
- features built primarily to make the platform appear larger.

Existing surfaces may remain live and receive essential maintenance, security corrections, legal corrections, and serious defect repairs. They are not active growth workstreams.

## 6. WHAT CONTINUES

Authorized because it protects or enables revenue:

- platform recovery and continuity until canonical application and database state are proven sufficiently safe for controlled development;
- payment and order integrity;
- business inventory activation;
- sponsor fulfillment and reporting;
- sales and customer operations.

Recovery work must not become an endless research program.

## 7. THE REVENUE GATE

Before any task begins, it must satisfy at least one condition:

1. helps BWE acquire a paying customer within 90 days;
2. helps retain or renew a paying customer;
3. helps fulfill and prove a paid customer outcome;
4. is necessary for payment integrity, security, compliance, trust, support, or operational continuity;
5. removes a confirmed blocker from the approved revenue funnel.

If a task satisfies none of these conditions, do not build it. Place it in the deferred backlog, state why it was deferred, and do not consume the active workstream with it.

## 8. THE BWE REVENUE FUNNEL

All active work must support this funnel:

1. identify a qualified business, sponsor, or service prospect;
2. establish contactability;
3. conduct outreach;
4. schedule a conversation or demonstration;
5. present one approved offer;
6. receive payment or a signed agreement;
7. onboard the customer;
8. deliver the promised service or campaign;
9. measure the result;
10. report the result;
11. request renewal, upgrade, referral, or testimonial.

The platform must help move customers through this funnel. The platform must not become a substitute for performing the funnel.

## 9. EXECUTION PHASES

### Phase Zero: Controlled Transition

Immediate through first safe stopping point:

- finish minimum required recovery and continuity work;
- identify canonical repository and deployment path;
- preserve live database;
- document current application and data baseline;
- freeze non-revenue expansion;
- create the first revenue scorecard;
- classify the existing backlog.

Required backlog classes:

- Revenue Critical;
- Trust or Payment Critical;
- Customer Fulfillment;
- Maintenance;
- Deferred;
- Retired.

### Phase One: Revenue Activation

First 30 days after Phase Zero. Judge by prospects contacted, customers closed, money collected, services delivered, and results documented.

### Phase Two: Repeatability

Days 31 through 90. Purpose: prove the process can repeat.

### Phase Three: Controlled Growth

Months 4 through 6.

### Phase Four: Delegation and Expansion of the Proven Engine

Months 7 through 9.

### Phase Five: Scale Readiness

Months 10 through 12.

## 10. OWNER-TIME PROTECTION

Thomas Hooker remains employed full time. Owner time should focus on major decisions, sales conversations, sponsor relationships, institutional partnerships, customer discovery, pricing, offer approval, key hiring, financial review, and high-value relationship building.

Every owner decision request must include:

- exact decision;
- why it matters;
- available evidence;
- recommended choice;
- consequence of delay;
- whether the decision blocks revenue.

For this period BlackForge is:

- revenue-enablement engineer;

- payment-integrity owner;
- business-activation systems builder;

## 12. ENGINEERING AND DATA RULES

Continue these mandatory standards:

- do not expose or print secrets;
- do not modify environment values without explicit approval;
- do not mutate production data without backup, written reason, exact scope, rollback path, and authorization;
- do not fabricate evidence;
- do not estimate live counts when exact counts can be obtained;
- do not treat unpaid orders as revenue;
- do not treat test payments as production revenue;
- do not create duplicate payment architectures;
- do not create duplicate authentication systems;
- do not create unnecessary replacement systems where canonical systems exist;
- do not perform sweeping refactors without proven revenue, security, or stability need;
- do not use placeholder dashboards as proof of completion;
- do not declare a workflow complete without end-to-end evidence;
- do not change production solely because a local screen appears correct;
- do not confuse code existence with operational readiness;
- do not confuse database volume with customer traction.

Every material implementation must include, where applicable, source evidence, local proof, API proof, database proof, payment proof, UI proof, regression proof, deployment proof, and operational handoff.

## 13. THE WEEKLY BWE SCORECARD

Maintain one weekly scorecard covering revenue, customers, sales pipeline, sponsorship, readiness services, business inventory activation, customer outcomes, and platform health.

## 14. WEEKLY EXECUTIVE REPORT

At the end of each operating week answer:

1. What money was collected?
2. Which customers paid?
3. What value did BWE deliver?
4. What customer outcomes were documented?
5. What renewed, upgraded, cancelled, or failed?
6. What is blocking the next sale?
7. What technical work directly improved revenue or customer delivery?
8. What work was deferred because it failed the Revenue Gate?
9. What owner decisions are required?
10. What is the single most important priority for the next seven days?

## 15. DEFINITION OF SUCCESS

BWE is successful only if it proves people will pay, customers receive measurable value, customers remain or renew, sponsors receive credible fulfillment evidence, services can be delivered profitably, sales can repeat, fulfillment can repeat, reporting can repeat, operations can be delegated, and revenue can grow without continuously adding unrelated products.
