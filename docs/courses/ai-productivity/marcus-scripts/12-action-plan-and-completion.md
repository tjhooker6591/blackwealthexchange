# Lesson 12 — Capstone: Your 30-Day AI Action Plan
Presenter: Marcus · Target length: about 3–4 minutes · Paste everything below the line into HeyGen.

---

You made it to the final lesson. Take a second and look at what you've built.

A task inventory with a real baseline. A prompt card. Email templates with approval lines. A triage matrix. A meeting summary method. A proposal fact-check. A reporting habit. A marketing claim check. An AI use policy. And a workflow with a scorecard. That's a lot. Now let's turn it into a plan you'll actually follow.

Here's how the thirty days break down.

Week one: choose one low-risk task, and record your baseline for a full week. Real minutes, real mistakes.

Week two: write and test your prompts and your checking rules, using made-up examples.

Week three: try the workflow on approved real cases. Write down every problem, and fix the steps.

Week four: run a controlled pilot. Fill in your scorecard, and make a decision: keep going, change course, or stop.

A strong plan has a few things in common. It names an owner, one person who is responsible. It uses only approved information. It includes the prompts you'll use. It sets a review checkpoint, so you know who approves what. It states a clear goal for time saved and for quality. And it says when you'll stop.

A weak plan promises guaranteed profit, skips testing, or has nobody responsible.

Let me give you an example. A home cleaning company wants to reply to quote requests faster, without pricing mistakes. The owner is responsible, and the office assistant drafts the replies. The only approved information is the public price sheet and the service list; no customer addresses go into prompts. They use three prompts: a quote reply, a follow-up, and a "we're booked" message. The owner approves every reply that mentions a price. The goal is to cut reply time from two hours to thirty minutes, with zero pricing errors. And the stop condition: any pricing error that reaches a customer pauses the pilot for review.

Simple, clear, and safe.
