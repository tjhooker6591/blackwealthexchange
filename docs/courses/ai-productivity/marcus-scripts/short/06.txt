Welcome to Lesson 6: meetings and action items. Have you ever left a meeting and realized everyone remembers it differently? A written summary fixes that, and Copilot can write one from your notes in under a minute.

Ask for the same four sections every time. A short summary of what was discussed. The decisions that were actually made. Action items, each with an owner and a due date. And open questions.

Keep discussion and decisions separate. "We talked about raising prices" is not the same as "We decided to raise prices on June first."

Here's the biggest risk. If nobody volunteered for a task, Copilot may still assign it to someone who sounds likely. So tell it: if an action has no owner, write UNASSIGNED. If it has no date, write NO DATE. Then your team fills the gaps.

And always get everyone's permission before you record a meeting or upload a transcript.

Try the sample notes on this page. Maya checks stock by Thursday. The poster has no owner. The price stays twenty-five dollars. Delivery partners is an open question. If Copilot gets any of that wrong, tighten your template.
